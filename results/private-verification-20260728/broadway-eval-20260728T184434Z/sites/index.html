Broadway Through the Years

A journey through the evolution of American musical theater, from the golden age to the modern blockbuster.

Broadway has always been a mirror to society, reflecting our hopes, fears, and dreams. From the optimistic escapism of the post-war era to the gritty realism of modern rock operas, the theater district has reinvented itself every few decades.
